First release.

- Floating bubble showing the wind relative to your direction of travel
- Heading from the GPS course, with the device compass taking over when you stop
- Live wind, gusts and temperature from Open-Meteo, no account needed
- Head/tail and crosswind components, in km/h, mph, m/s or knots
- Adjustable refresh interval, bubble size, opacity and position
- English and French, no tracking, no advertising
